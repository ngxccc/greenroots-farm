# Glossary — Shared Contracts

Cross-app vocabulary for GreenRoots Farm. Terms here are authoritative for `packages/shared` and MUST be reused by every app.

## Domain Purpose

GreenRoots Farm sells farm-grown produce through a storefront and operates production, packing, delivery, and subscriptions through a backoffice.

## Terms

## Invariants

- Money is stored and calculated as integer VND. Never use floating point for money.
- A batch is sellable only while its status is `in_stock` or `near_expiry` and its expiry timestamp is in the future.
- An order can transition only along the transitions defined by `OrderStatus` (`pending_payment` -> `pending_confirmation` -> `confirmed` -> `packing` -> `shipping` -> `delivered` -> `completed`, or `cancelled` from pre-shipping states); `completed` and `cancelled` are terminal, and every order in `pending_confirmation`, `confirmed`, `packing`, or `shipping` holds active FEFO `batch_allocations` (`ADR-0038`).
- A subscription has exactly one active delivery-day selection at a time.
- A season transitions linearly along `planning -> active -> harvested -> completed` (`completed` is terminal), and each plot allows at most one `active` season at a time.
- A farming log is strictly append-only (never updated or deleted), and any correction linkage must reference an existing log within the same season.
- A waste log deducts batch available stock only upon independent administrative approval (never while pending, and never self-reviewed by its reporter); `approved` and `rejected` waste logs are terminal.
- Delivery quotes are deliverable only when road distance is within the farm's Delivery Radius (`maxRadiusKm`); subscription deliveries charge 0 VND within the Subscription Free Radius and standard shipping fees between the Subscription Free Radius and the Delivery Radius.
- Farm shipping configuration requires positive Delivery Radius (`maxRadiusKm`) and Subscription Free Radius (`subscriptionFreeRadiusKm`), and both Subscription Free Radius and standard free-km threshold (`freeKmThreshold`) must be less than or equal to Delivery Radius (`ADR-0034`).
- Shared enums and constants are defined once in `packages/shared` and imported by apps. Apps MUST NOT redeclare them.
- Mutating commands (checkout, payments, refunds) must be guarded with an `Idempotency-Key`; replays with matching payloads return cached responses, while mismatched payloads are rejected with `422`.
- Media uploads are restricted to $\le 5\text{MB}$ for images and $\le 15\text{MB}$ for videos; oversized uploads are rejected at the signature boundary.
- Access tokens have a 15-minute TTL; refresh tokens have a 7-day TTL and are hashed with SHA-256 before database persistence.
- Single logout invalidates only the calling device's session, whereas password changes, password resets, and `logout-all` trigger instant account-wide revocation in Redis for 15 minutes.
